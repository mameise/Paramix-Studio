# V3Emu

The Tamagotchi Connection V3 on the Tamagotchi Paradise. No ROM here: bring your own (512 KB).

- `src/core/v3cpu.c`, `v3cpu.h`: Sunplus SPLB32 (6502 with banked ROM, timers, 1 kHz time base, STOP, buzzer)
  and the HT24LC08 EEPROM, ported from BrickEmuPy by azya52 (CC0), `cores/SPLB32.py` and
  `peripherals/HT24LC08.py`. Time in ticks of 1/128 cycle, so the 32768 Hz clock is a whole number of ticks.
- `src/core/v3ops.inc`: the 256 instructions, generated by `gen/py2c.py` from `SPLB32.py`.
- `src/core/v3lcd.inc`: which bit of the LCD RAM lights which of the 32 x 30 dots, taken from the face
  `assets/Tamagotchi_v3.svg` of BrickEmuPy (only the positions).
- `src/app/main.c`: the Paradise program. Settings block of V1Emu ("PMX1"), the Studio tells the two apart
  by the ROM size. Save: session (4 KB), then settings and the toy's EEPROM (4 KB).
- `host/v3run.c`: runs a ROM on the computer, writes the screen as PGM (`TRACE`, `SHOTS`).
- `test/devv3.py`: the Paradise program in Unicorn (screen, keys, flash); `test/mkimg.py` makes a test image;
  `test/profv3.py` counts instructions per function. `POKE=ms:symbol+off:byte` in devv3 writes a byte once, `PMB=seconds` puts the Paradise's time in the mailbox,
`DUMP=symbol+off:len` prints memory at the end. `host/v3run` takes `RX=t:hex` (bytes into the receiver) and prints sent bytes.

Build: `sh src/app/build.sh` (clang, lld) gives `v3.bin`.

Clock (V3_CLK_RETRY, test37): a standing clock (the toy started from its own save and asks for the time) is looked at
again every 5 s for 10 minutes and set as soon as it runs; the settings line "Clock" shows AUTO OK, AUTO ! (stands),
AUTO - (no time from the Paradise) or AUTO ? (written but off).

Switches in the program: `V3_CLOCK` (the Paradise's time at the start), `V3_LINK` (infrared over the contacts), `V3_ICONS` (the ten icons, segments 0_7, 1_0..1_3 above and 6_3..6_0, 5_7 below; pictures in
`src/app/icons_v3.h`, made by `gen/icons.py` from mameise's PNGs; a missing one is taken from T7Emu).

The core's infrared (`v3_ir_rx`, `v3_ir_tx`) was checked against CON_V3_IR.py with the same script of port writes
and clock steps (sent bytes and receiver level the same).

Switches in the core: `V3_FREESTANDING` (no libc), `V3_BATCH` (0: no skipping while stopped, for comparing).
